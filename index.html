Why Saudi Arabia

A market with serious opportunity and serious setup requirements.

Saudi Arabia continues to attract international companies across professional services, technology, industry, trade, logistics, and investment-led sectors. The opportunity is significant, but the foundation matters: the right license, registrations, and government setup must come first.

What must be mapped early

  • Business activity and license category
  • Foreign ownership and shareholder documents
  • Commercial Registration and entity details
  • Tax registration and early compliance steps
  • Government portal access and authority records
  • Sequencing requirements before operations begin
